## Analysis

### The Elephant in the Room: Linear Equations in One Variable — 0/5

This is the most alarming result across all 7 tests. On Bluebook, this subfield was routine. On OnePrep's harder calibration, these become:
- Multi-step word problems with unusual framing
- Equations where the "linear" structure is disguised inside absolute values, ratios, or unit conversions
- Problems that look simple but have subtle traps (distributing negatives, fractional coefficients, "no solution" vs "all solutions" conditions)

**This is now the #1 priority for the remaining 10 days.**

### What Held Up

- **Geometry: 6/6 (100%)** — bulletproof across all test types. No drilling needed.
- **Nonlinear functions: 5/5** — pattern recognition is strong.
- **Equivalent expressions: 2/2** — solid algebra manipulation.
- **Systems of linear equations: 3/3** — CW simultaneous solver is working.

### What Slipped

- **Nonlinear equations/systems: 3/5 (60%)** — this has been 3/5 or 3/6 for multiple tests. It's the persistent Module 2 ceiling.
- **Percentages: 1/2** — needs more practice with percent change, compound percent, "percent of" vs "percent greater than" traps.
- **Inference/margin of error: 1/2** — review confidence interval interpretation and sample size effects.
