## What the SAT Tests

- Creating and solving quadratic and exponential equations
- Interpreting nonlinear expressions and equations
- Understanding the relationship between zeros, factors, and graphs
- Rewriting expressions using structure (factoring, completing the square)
- Operations with polynomials and rational expressions

## Key Skills

- Factor quadratics: $ax^2 + bx + c$
- Use the quadratic formula: $x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$
- Recognize exponential growth/decay: $y = a \cdot b^x$
- Simplify radicals and rational expressions
- Understand function notation: $f(x)$, $f(g(x))$, transformations
