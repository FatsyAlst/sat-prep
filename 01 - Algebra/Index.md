## What the SAT Tests

- Creating, solving, and interpreting linear equations and inequalities
- Creating, solving, and interpreting systems of linear equations
- Understanding and interpreting connections between algebraic and graphical representations

## Key Skills

- Translate word problems into equations/inequalities
- Solve for variables, including when equations have no solution or infinite solutions
- Interpret slope and y-intercept in context
- Graph lines and systems, identify solutions visually
- Work with absolute value equations and inequalities
